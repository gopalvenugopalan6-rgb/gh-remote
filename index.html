Connection refused, or nothing happens? Trust the TV’s certificate first →

LG TVs secure port 3001 with a certificate they signed themselves, which your browser does not trust by default. From an https:// page the browser silently blocks the connection instead of asking you.

One-time fix, per device:

  1. Open https://TV-IP:3001 in this same browser.
  2. You will get a privacy warning. Tap Advanced → Proceed anyway.
  3. The page will look broken or blank. That is fine — the exception is now stored.
  4. Come back here and press Connect.
Don’t know the IP? Scan the network

Scans .1–.254 for anything answering on port 3001. A browser cannot see your network directly, so this measures response timing — it is a good guess, not proof.
